# The public services behind chapter 7

The `mock` profile answers with the responses in this folder, recorded from the live services on 5 and 6 November 2026. The answers that change with time, such as forecasts, prices and bus positions, were recorded on 5 November between 22:20 and 22:40 UTC. Each service below is credited as its publisher asks, or as the source when its metadata does not name a licence. The recordings are reproduced as the services sent them.

| Service | Publisher | Address | Terms |
|---|---|---|---|
| Fuel prices (Preços dos Combustíveis Online) | DGEG, Direção-Geral de Energia e Geologia | https://precoscombustiveis.dgeg.gov.pt | The portal says its information is free and may be used freely, if the reuse names DGEG as the source, gives the date and time of the last update, and reproduces the data faithfully. Every price in this schema carries its update time and names DGEG. |
| Local lodging register and tourism layers | Turismo de Portugal | https://dadosabertos.turismodeportugal.pt | CC-BY-4.0, as the metadata of each layer says. |
| SNIAmb, the environmental data of Portugal | APA, Agência Portuguesa do Ambiente | https://sniambgeoogc.apambiente.pt | The layers credit Agência Portuguesa do Ambiente, I.P.; credit APA as the source. |
| Transparency portal of the National Health Service | SNS, Serviço Nacional de Saúde | https://transparencia.sns.gov.pt | Its metadata does not name a licence; credit the SNS as the source. |
| Indicators | INE, Statistics Portugal | https://www.ine.pt | Credit INE as the source. |
| Datasets | Eurostat, the statistical office of the European Union | https://ec.europa.eu/eurostat | Eurostat's copyright notice allows reuse with acknowledgement of the source. |
| Forecasts, warnings, earthquakes and observations | IPMA, Instituto Português do Mar e da Atmosfera | https://api.ipma.pt | Credit IPMA as the source. |
| Urban platform (FIWARE) | Associação Porto Digital | https://broker.fiware.urbanplatform.portodigital.pt | Credit Porto Digital as the source. |
| ROPI, the register of points of interest of Porto | Associação Porto Digital | https://api.ropi.porto.digital | Its documentation asks for a token, and its reads answer without one; credit Porto Digital as the source. |

The recordings leave out personal data. Every name in the local lodging register reads "(left out of the recording)", because the register names many units after their owners, and the points of interest and parks keep their websites without their telephones, faxes and emails. The live services return them.
